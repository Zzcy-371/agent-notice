## Research and engineering

### [Standalone LLM and a Pre-specified Agentic Pipeline for Explaining ICU Mortality Predictions: a Feasibility Study on the eICU Demo Dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26109)
- This feasibility study compares a standalone LLM with a four-step agentic pipeline (MCP-based tool calling for data interpretation, guideline checking, and final explanation) on the eICU Demo Dataset (2,353 ICU stays, 8.1% mortality), showing XGBoost achieves AUROC 0.855 and AUPRC 0.332.
- On a 38-case explanation subset, the agentic pipeline eliminates explicit outcome leakage (0 cases vs. 1 for standalone), and in SHAP-overlapping cases it improves guideline grounding (0.762 vs. 0.143) and value specificity (0.236 vs. 0.143), while standalone shows higher SHAP alignment (Jaccard 0.171 vs. 0.077) and direction consistency (92.9% vs. 78.6%).
- Clinically, agentic decomposition enhances safety-relevant grounding and patient-specific detail but requires pairing with attribution-based checks in high-stakes risk explanation, highlighting the need for hybrid RAG and multi-agent designs in ICU mortality prediction.

### [PICasso: An AI-Enabled Design Framework for Autonomous Optimization of Silicon Photonic Devices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26113)
- PICasso integrates NL-to-YAML-to-GDS generation with PDK-aware knowledge and MCP-like structured constraints, enabling automated synthesis, placement, routing, and DRC/LVS validation with SAX-based simulation.
- Leveraging RAG and tool calling, PICasso injects domain knowledge and invokes simulation tools to guide optimization, improving structural Spec@3 to 92.7% and functional Spec@3 to 52% on high-complexity circuits.
- In a multi-agent design pipeline, simulation feedback reduces circuit insertion loss by 1.74 dB (from 4.98 dB to 3.25 dB), transforming LLMs into practical PIC design agents with competitive runtimes.

### [CIFQA：一种用于金融问答的确定性工具化多智能体LLM框架](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26114)
- CIFQA通过将语言理解与数值执行分离，结合确定性Python工具和专用智能体（如查询解释、路由、参数提取、计算规划和响应生成），实现了金融计算密集型问答的高精度。
- 在固定存款查询基准上，CIFQA在计算密集型查询上达到95.54%的准确率，总体准确率90.87%，显著优于直接使用LLM的基线，消融实验表明精确利率查找、期限计算、滚动年调整和提前支取逻辑等确定性组件是关键。
- 使用17B开源模型作为CIFQA骨干，其性能优于使用相同金融信息评估的更大规模前沿模型，表明架构设计比模型规模对数值可靠性更重要，且该框架可推广至其他计算密集型金融推理任务。

### [The Artificial Experimentalist: Discovery and Control of Self-Organizing Phenomena with Autotelic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26116)
- 提出基于自决强化学习的闭环框架，使智能体通过MCP自主采样多样化目标并学习目标条件策略，以最小局部扰动干预复杂系统（如Lenia连续元胞自动机），实现涌现现象的高效发现与控制。
- CARL智能体在多种更新规则下以更高比率发现稳定孤子，并可稀疏干预控制其运动方向，同时通过RAG整合多源知识，借助工具调用与多智能体协作，从随机初始状态泛化至分布外条件。
- 训练后的智能体支持人类通过高层方向指令实时引导孤子穿越迷宫，将抽象指令翻译为底层干预，展示了人工实验智能体在自主或人机协同下操控复杂系统涌现行为的能力。
