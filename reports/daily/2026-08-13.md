## Research and engineering

### [Closed-Loop LLM Co-Pilots for Digital Agriculture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09949)
- 本研究将大型语言模型（LLM）从数据分析扩展到自主AI引导实验，利用49通道植物传感器网络（多光谱、电化学和介电模态）提供实时自然语言解读，实现从人工分析到自主控制的转变。
- 闭环架构直接连接AI与生物系统，通过LLM处理生物物理数据并触发硬件执行器，优化微气候、执行表型协议或诱导胁迫场景；在垂直农场和单株系统中验证，成功解码植物生理的微宏波动。
- 在生产规模部署中，LLM智能体协同执行多参数优化，在2小时间隔内调节全光谱、450nm和660nm光照；相比周期控制，最短时间模式将生产周期缩短35%，能量优化模式节能18%，并自主发现暗诱导叶绿素积累策略，节能67.9%，展示了LLM作为数字农业自主副驾驶的潜力。

### [SPOTting the Future: Lookahead Explanations for Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09967)
- SPOT is a model-agnostic, sampling-based framework for interpreting DRL policies, constructing an interpretable finite-horizon tree to empirically represent action preferences and downstream evolution.
- It provides formal guarantees of asymptotic recovery of the most probable action and characterizes disagreement under high-entropy policies.
- Demonstrated in SUMO-RL traffic-signal control, it reveals downstream behaviors invisible to single-timestep feature-attribution methods, enhancing MCP, RAG, tool calling, and multi-agent interpretability.

### [Automating and Scaling Behavioral Scientific Research on AI Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10030)
- Introduces AEROBAT, the first multi-agent system to automate behavioral scientific research on AI agents, integrating MCP, RAG, and tool calling to execute a full research pipeline from hypothesis generation to report writing.
- Demonstrates large-scale automated experimentation: across 12 target behaviors, AEROBAT generated and tested 79 hypotheses via 1,240 controlled experiments and 23,512 simulation rounds, finding statistical evidence for 26 hypotheses.
- Shows that automated research can complement manual efforts, highlighting the potential of multi-agent AI to scale scientific inquiry and accelerate discovery in behavioral studies.

### [MESA: Task-Adaptive Multi-Structure Evidence Selection for Long-Horizon Agent Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10108)
- MESA addresses long-horizon agent memory by dynamically selecting and fusing a query-adaptive subset from five complementary structure views of each trajectory, outperforming fixed-set and single-structure baselines.
- It uses harness optimization with prior-guided search and UCB-guided scheduling to learn from end-to-end answer-level feedback, despite weak supervision, balancing exploration and exploitation.
- On AMA-Bench, MESA improves accuracy by 8.5% over the strongest baseline while reducing evidence tokens by 41% compared to using all structures, demonstrating efficient multi-memory integration.
